6. SmartChoice AI recommendation chatbot

Ask for products from the included India packaged-food catalogue, such as “Recommend a low-sugar vegetarian snack under ₹100” or “Suggest high-protein options and avoid peanuts.” The chatbot uses your dietary profile, the recommendation model and retrieved guidance.

SmartChoice: Hello! Tell me your dietary goal, food preferences, ingredients to avoid or budget.

With OPENAI_API_KEY, the chatbot can generate a grounded natural-language answer. Without it, the local catalogue recommender and RAG fallback still work.
